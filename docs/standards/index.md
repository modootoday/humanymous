# Standards research

**Diátaxis quadrant:** Reference. **Audience:** implementers, standards participants and reviewers.

These are independent humanymous project proposals. They have not been submitted,
adopted or endorsed by the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF), World Wide Web
Consortium (W3C), WHATWG or a registry. humanymous remains a reference
implementation; internally consistent real-browser or human-assisted automation
cannot reliably be distinguished from legitimate traffic by these observations.

The [Draft v0 collection](draft-v0/index.md) explores interoperable evidence and
decision exchange between servers and adapters. It does not standardize a
human-detection algorithm or require a vendor blocklist.

Draft v0 is under development. Publication of an immutable revision and external
submission are separate milestones; no external review or interoperability result
is claimed by the existence of these pages.
