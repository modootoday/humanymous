# Data-protection review guide

**Diátaxis quadrant:** How-to. **Audience:** privacy reviewers and service owners.

A reference implementation is not a compliance certification. The Rust migration
has not completed operational privacy or erasure qualification.

Map each observation to its purpose, collection point, retention period, access
role and deletion behavior. Distinguish durable security audit from optional
analytics. Exclude raw forms, passwords, clipboard contents and screenshots from
routine collection. Pseudonymous identifiers may still be personal data.

Review recovery for challenged visitors, alternative access, shared devices and
assistive technology. Define subject-request handling without weakening audit
continuity. Erasure and other destructive administration require dual control.
Use the [data inventory](../reference/data-processing-inventory.md) and
[privacy assessment companion](../explanation/dpia-companion.md) as preparation,
then obtain review appropriate to your deployment and jurisdiction.
