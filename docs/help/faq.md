# Visitor questions

**Diátaxis quadrant:** Reference. **Audience:** site visitors.

## Does a challenge mean I did something wrong?

No. Missing evidence, compatibility problems and service faults can also cause
additional checks. A verdict describes a request outcome, not your identity.

## Must I move a mouse quickly?

No. Recovery must not depend on pointer richness or speed. Request an
[accessible alternative](challenge-accessibility.md) if needed.

## Who can restore access?

The operator of the site you are visiting controls its policy. Contact that
operator with a support reference, not your password or session cookie.

## Does this identify every automated browser?

No. Coherent browser automation and human-assisted use remain a deliberate
detection limit. The project is a reference implementation under migration.
