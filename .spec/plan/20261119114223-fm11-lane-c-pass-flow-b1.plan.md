## Accessibility conflict (owner decision needed)

`realEventOK` rejects a Pass on interaction richness and timing regularity,
and the `applyPassBehavior` tells raise risk on timing and pointer evidence.
Both gate Pass on motor richness or speed, which the accessibility hard rule
forbids. They are ported exactly and pinned by goldens, but the served flow
keeps them off unless the owner enables them.
